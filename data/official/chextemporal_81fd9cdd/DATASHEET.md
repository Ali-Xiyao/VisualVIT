# Datasheet for CheXTemporal

Following [Gebru et al., *Datasheets for Datasets* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010).

## Motivation

**Purpose.** Enable training and evaluation of vision–language models on
longitudinal chest-radiograph progression and anatomy-aligned
localization. Existing public chest-X-ray corpora (CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR,
ReXGradient) provide single-time-point labels but no paired (current,
prior) supervision needed to learn temporal change. CheXTemporal closes
that gap with (i) silver progression labels derived from radiology
reports by a vision-language model and (ii) a 1,787-row gold evaluation
set with expert bounding boxes.

**Creators / funding.** Anonymous (NeurIPS 2025 submission).
De-anonymized at camera-ready.

## Composition

**Instances.** Each silver instance is a (study, finding) annotation
where the patient has a verified in-corpus prior study and both prior
and current images were on disk at release time. Each gold instance is
an expert-annotated (study, finding) annotation drawn from the same
parent corpora.

**Counts.** 282,214 silver pair × finding examples (34,296 unique
patients) and 1,787 gold pair × finding annotations (197 unique
patients).

**Sampling.** Silver is restricted to studies with a verified prior
under the matching policy described in the paper, and to reports
MedGemma was able to parse without error. Gold is a stratified expert
sample.

**Per-instance content.** A finding name, 5-way progression label,
anatomy phrase, MedGemma's free-text reasoning + evidence quotes, and
relative paths to the parent images and (when available) the
anatomy-aligned mask JSON. All textual annotations live in the parquet
tables; COCO-format anatomy masks are shipped as `silver_masks.zip`.

**Targets.** `progression` (in both silver and gold parquets), plus the
disease name. For localization: gold has expert bounding boxes; silver
has automatic anatomy-aligned masks (CheXpert + MIMIC-CXR only).

**Missing data.** ReXGradient instances have no anatomy masks (CXAS not
yet run on that corpus). Some `evidence` fields are empty when MedGemma
could not extract a verbatim quote. MIMIC-CXR Findings sections are
often empty (Impression-only reports).

**Relationships.** `patient_id` joins instances belonging to the same
patient; `study_id_curr`/`study_id_prev` join instances forming the
same image pair; `(dataset, patient_id, study_id_curr)` joins
`silver_findings.parquet` to `silver_studies.parquet` for report
context.

**Splits.** Source-based — gold is held-out evaluation, silver is for
training. Within silver, no train/val split is prescribed (we use
patient-disjoint random sampling).

**Errors / noise.** Silver labels are model-generated and contain
residual noise. Baseline VLMs achieve ~21–28% top-1 progression
accuracy on the gold split, so silver labels are useful as supervision
but should not be treated as ground truth.

**Self-contained?** Annotations only — images must be obtained
separately under each parent corpus's license.

**Confidential / personal information.** All instances derive from
de-identified parent corpora. Patient IDs follow each corpus's
de-identification protocol. Free-text reports were de-identified by
the parent corpora's curators. No re-identification attempts were made.

**Potentially harmful content.** Reports may describe serious medical
findings, including emergencies and end-of-life conditions.

## Collection process

**How acquired.** Silver: programmatic extraction from existing
radiology reports in CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, and ReXGradient. Gold:
expert annotation on a sampled subset of patient timelines.

**Mechanisms.**
- Each Impression section was sentence-split and labelled by MedGemma
  27B as `static` or `dynamic`.
- For each (study, finding) pair, MedGemma was prompted with the
  current and prior impressions to produce a `progression` label, an
  `anatomy` phrase, a free-text rationale, and supporting `evidence`
  quotes.
- Anatomy masks were generated by running CXAS on each CheXpert/MIMIC
  frontal image and filtering to anatomies relevant to each finding.
- Pair construction: chronological for CheXpert; impression-text
  matching for MIMIC and ReXGradient.

**Annotators.** Researchers listed in the paper at de-anonymization;
gold annotators are board-certified or attending radiologists.

**Timeframe.** Silver and gold labels were generated in 2025. Parent
corpora span 2002–2017 (MIMIC-CXR / CheXpert) and an overlapping range
for ReXGradient.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labelling

- Reports were sentence-split with a punctuation-and-newline-aware
  splitter, then labelled by MedGemma.
- Anatomy phrases were normalized via a hand-curated mapping
  (e.g. `LLL`, `left lower lobe`, `lower left lung` → `left lower lung`).
- Lateral views were filtered out; only frontal views are included.
- Findings without a usable prior or whose images were missing on disk
  were filtered out.

The textual MedGemma outputs are released as parquet tables (the
canonical form). The COCO-format anatomy masks are released in their
original form as `silver_masks.zip`, referenced by the
`silver_mask_path` column of `silver_findings.parquet`.

## Uses

**Already used for.** The introducing paper evaluates several
baselines on (a) 5-way progression classification and (b)
anatomy-aligned heatmap localization (CNR + Pointing Game).

**Other potential uses.** Single-image findings detection (using
`finding` and `anatomy` without the temporal pair); sentence-level
static/dynamic classification; fine-tuning chest-X-ray VLMs with
paired supervision; sentence retrieval against image features.

**Caveats.** Silver labels come from a single VLM (MedGemma 27B) and
inherit its idiosyncrasies, including a tendency to default to "new"
when context is ambiguous. Account for this class skew.

**Should NOT be used for.** Clinical decision-making for individual
patients. Re-identification or any attempt to undo the parent corpora's
de-identification. Commercial use (see license).

## Distribution

**Where.** HuggingFace Datasets at `anonaccount107240/CheXTemporal`
(anonymized for review).

**How.** Five Parquet tables (three silver, two gold) plus one ZIP
archive of anatomy masks, with this datasheet, a `README.md` (HF
dataset card), and a `LICENSE` file. A persistent DOI will be minted
via HuggingFace's DOI integration.

**When.** At submission for review; publicly at acceptance / camera-ready.

**License.** Annotations under **CC-BY-NC 4.0**. Parent images retain
their respective licenses (CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, ReXGradient) and are
NOT redistributed. Users must independently comply with each parent
corpus's access agreement to obtain images.
